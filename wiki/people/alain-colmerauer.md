# Alain Colmerauer

**Who / when.** French computer scientist (1941–2017, general knowledge) at the University of Aix-Marseille.

**Contribution to AI.**
- Created **[[prolog]]** (*programmation en logique*) with **Philippe Roussel** in **Marseille, 1972**, on theory from [[robert-kowalski]] ([[slides-xx-logic-programming-prolog]], slide 8).
- Purpose: **parsing natural language** — Prolog clauses were first intended as context-free grammar rules (Colmerauer et al. 1973; Roussel 1975) ([[russell-norvig-aima]] Ch. 9 notes). This survives as definite clause grammars (slide 8).
- Prolog executes [[horn-clauses]] by [[backward-chaining]] / [[sld-resolution]]; it became popular in Europe and Japan and was the basis of Japan's Fifth Generation project (1981) ([[russell-norvig-aima]] §1.3.4).
