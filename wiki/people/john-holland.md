# John H. Holland

**Who / when.** American computer scientist at the University of Michigan (1929–2015, general knowledge).

**Contribution to AI.**
- Proposed **[[genetic-algorithms]]** in **1975** "as a general model that could adapt to any process" ([[slides-04-optimization]], slide 2) — book *Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems* (general knowledge). R&N: Holland "championed genetic algorithms, both as a useful optimization tool and as a method to expand our understanding of adaptation" ([[russell-norvig-aima]] Ch. 4 notes).
- **Schema theorem / building blocks**: explains why GAs work → [[schema-theory-and-building-blocks]].
- The course reading is his popular 1992 *Scientific American* article "Genetic Algorithms" ([[holland-1992-genetic-algorithms]]); slide 5 of [[slides-04-optimization]] labels GAs "John Holland – 1992" after that article, while slide 2 gives the 1975 origin.
