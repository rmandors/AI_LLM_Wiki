# Derviş Karaboğa

**Who / when.** Turkish computer engineer at Erciyes University (general knowledge). First described ABC in a 2005 technical report (general knowledge); the widely cited journal version is Karaboga & Basturk (**2007**), the date used in the course ([[slides-04-optimization]], slide 16; [[russell-norvig-aima]] Ch. 4 notes).

**Contribution to AI.**
- **[[artificial-bee-colony]]** (ABC): swarm-intelligence optimiser imitating honeybee foraging ([[slides-04-optimization]], slide 16).
- Three bee roles: **employed** and **onlooker** bees exploit, **scout** bees explore; control parameters: number of food sources (= employed or onlooker bees), the *limit*, and the maximum number of cycles (slides 17–18) — see [[exploration-vs-exploitation]].
