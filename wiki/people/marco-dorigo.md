# Marco Dorigo

**Who / when.** Italian computer scientist (b. 1961, general knowledge); proposed ant algorithms in his 1992 PhD thesis at Politecnico di Milano (general knowledge); later at Université Libre de Bruxelles (general knowledge).

**Contribution to AI.**
- **[[ant-colony-optimization]]**: the course reading is Dorigo, Maniezzo & Colorni, "Ant System: Optimization by a Colony of Cooperating Agents", *IEEE Trans. SMC-B* 26(1), Feb **1996** ([[dorigo-1996-ant-system]], p. 1). Key ideas: positive feedback, distributed computation and a constructive greedy heuristic, tested on the Traveling Salesman Problem and compared with tabu search and simulated annealing (abstract).
- [[slides-04-optimization]] slides 12–13: originally for combinatorial problems; ants reinforce good paths with pheromone, evaporation weakens poor ones.
