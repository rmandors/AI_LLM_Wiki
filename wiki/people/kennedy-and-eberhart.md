# James Kennedy and Russell Eberhart

**Who / when.** James Kennedy (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Washington DC) and Russell Eberhart (Purdue School of Engineering and Technology, Indianapolis), affiliations as printed on the paper ([[kennedy-eberhart-1995-pso]], p. 1). Kennedy is a social psychologist and Eberhart an electrical engineer (general knowledge).

**Contribution to AI.**
- Introduced **[[particle-swarm-optimization]]** (PSO) in **1995** for optimising continuous nonlinear functions; the method was "discovered through simulation of a simplified social model" ([[kennedy-eberhart-1995-pso]], abstract and §1).
- PSO is a stochastic optimiser "inspired by simulations of social behavior in birds and fish, as well as swarm theory"; particles share information about the best positions found ([[slides-04-optimization]], slide 8).
- Their paper relates PSO to artificial life and to [[genetic-algorithms]] and proposes neural-network training as an application ([[kennedy-eberhart-1995-pso]], abstract).
