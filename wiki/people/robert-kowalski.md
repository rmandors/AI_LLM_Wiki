# Robert Kowalski

**Who / when.** American-born British logician and computer scientist (b. 1941, general knowledge); at the University of Edinburgh in the early 1970s, then Imperial College London from 1975 (general knowledge).

**Contribution to AI.**
- Developed much of the **theory of logic programming**, working with Colmerauer ([[russell-norvig-aima]] Ch. 9 notes): the procedural interpretation of [[horn-clauses]] and SLD resolution ([[sld-resolution]]; general knowledge for the SLD attribution).
- **"Algorithm = Logic + Control" (1979)** — "your clauses are the logic; Prolog supplies the control" ([[slides-xx-logic-programming-prolog]], slide 2). The same slogan appears on [[slides-01-introduction-to-ai]] slide 9.
- Prolog was "born in Marseille in 1972 (Alain Colmerauer and Philippe Roussel), on theory from Robert Kowalski in Edinburgh" ([[slides-xx-logic-programming-prolog]], slide 8).

> [!note] Edinburgh vs Imperial
> The Prolog deck places Kowalski in Edinburgh; R&N say Imperial College London. Both are right for different periods — Edinburgh when Prolog was born (1972), Imperial from 1975 (general knowledge).
