# Frank Rosenblatt

**Who / when.** American psychologist at Cornell (1928–1971, general knowledge).

**Contribution to AI.**
- Proposed the **[[perceptron]]** in **1958** — "a computational model inspired by the biological operation of neurons in the brain" that stores learned knowledge in synaptic weights ([[slides-01-introduction-to-ai]], slide 8). Built the Mark I Perceptron hardware (general knowledge).
- Extended Hebb's learning ideas; book *Principles of Neurodynamics* (1962). The **perceptron convergence theorem** (Block et al. 1962) shows the learning rule finds a separator whenever one exists ([[russell-norvig-aima]] §1.3.2).
- Minsky & Papert's *Perceptrons* (1969) showed single-layer perceptrons cannot represent functions like XOR, and neural-network funding nearly vanished until backpropagation revived the field in the 1980s (§1.3.3, §1.3.5).
