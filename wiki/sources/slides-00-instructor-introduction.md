# Slides 00 — Instructor Introduction

**Bibliographic line.** Riofrío, D. *Instructor Introduction* (lecture slides, 5 slides). Artificial Intelligence, NRC 1651, USFQ, Fall 2026. File: `raw/Slides/00_Instructor Introduction.pptx`.

**What it is.** The first-day deck: who the instructor is, how to reach him, what the course covers and how it is graded. It is the authority for the syllabus units used across this wiki (see [[overview]]).

## Key points

- **Instructor** — [[daniel-riofrio]], "Computer Science and Applied and Computing Director" (slide 1). PhD in Computer Science from UNM; research area *Computer Optimization and Applied ML*; 8+ years at USFQ, 5+ years as Director of Computer Science (slide 2). Timeline on slide 2: PhD 2015, plus his beagles (Danielita 2018, SirPaul 2019, Ringo 2025).
- **Contact** (slide 3): email `driofrioa@usfq.edu.ec`; office **H317**; office hours **MTWR 14:30–16:00**; course channel **D2L**; Telegram **@danielriofrio**; expected reply time up to 24 h.
- **Course content — 10 units** (slide 4):
  1. History and foundations of AI → [[history-of-ai]], [[unit-01-history-review]]
  2. Intelligent agents and problem formulation → [[intelligent-agents]], [[problem-formulation]], [[unit-02-agents-review]]
  3. Heuristic search → [[informed-search]], [[a-star]], [[unit-03-search-review]]
  4. Adversarial search in games → [[adversarial-search]], [[minimax]], [[unit-04-games-review]]
  5. Knowledge, reasoning and planning → [[upcoming-topics]]
  6. Propositional and first-order logic: Prolog, unification, forward/backward chaining → [[propositional-logic]], [[first-order-logic]], [[prolog]], [[unification]], [[forward-chaining]], [[backward-chaining]]
  7. Uncertainty: probability, Bayes' theorem, naive Bayes → [[upcoming-topics]]
  8. Temporal reasoning and dynamic models: Markov → [[upcoming-topics]]
  9. Optimization and bio-inspired algorithms → [[genetic-algorithms]], [[particle-swarm-optimization]], [[ant-colony-optimization]], [[unit-09-optimization-review]]
  10. Machine learning and neural networks: perceptron; ethics and safety → [[perceptron]], [[upcoming-topics]]
